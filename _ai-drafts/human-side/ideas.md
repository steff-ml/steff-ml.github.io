> AI-written notes from the exponential-post interview (2026-11-13). Parked, not
> scheduled. Split off from the model-sourcing post, which now covers only the
> economic (vendor-side) argument.

## Why parked
- Many people have already written about the human side, and written it well.
  A post only makes sense with something of my own to add.
- I still need to explore other ways of working with AI first (e.g. systems design).
- My own experience with it is not publishable right now.

## The idea
- Human-side risks: deskilling; people who don't understand what they ship;
  people who start looking down on understanding things themselves; burnout and
  boreout as the job changes.
- The procurement alternatives from the model-sourcing post don't address these:
  a self-hosted model deskills you just as fast as Claude. Only a different way of
  working does.
- Possible approach: philosophical critiques + case studies from the social sciences.
- Link to the model-sourcing post: "own the core, rent the edge" applies at two
  levels. A company that rents its own processes; an engineer who rents their
  understanding.

## The interesting part: the solution (back pocket)
- Redefine your core identity.
- Fix problems you want to see fixed.
